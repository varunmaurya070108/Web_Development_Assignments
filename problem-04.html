Agra Weekend Destination Guide

Agra is a historic destination known for monumental architecture, Mughal-era heritage and distinctive North Indian food.

Attractions

The Taj Mahal is the city's best-known landmark. Agra Fort is another major heritage site, while Mehtab Bagh offers views toward the Taj Mahal across the Yamuna.

Culture

Agra's cultural identity reflects Mughal history, traditional crafts and a long connection with architecture and tourism.

Food

Visitors can try petha, Mughlai dishes, breads and other North Indian specialties. Local restaurants range from casual eateries to heritage dining spaces.

Best Time

October to March is generally the recommended season for a comfortable sightseeing trip because temperatures are usually more suitable for walking outdoors.

Travel Tips

Start major sightseeing early in the day so you have more time for the main monuments and can avoid the strongest afternoon heat.
