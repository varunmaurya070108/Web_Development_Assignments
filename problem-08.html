Article 1: Why HTML Still Matters

HTML provides the structure of a web page. Before styling and interaction are added, good HTML creates a clear foundation for content.

Learning the purpose of elements is more useful than memorizing tags without understanding them. A strong document hierarchy also helps future developers maintain the page.

Key idea: choose elements according to meaning and purpose.

Article 2: Building Better Study Projects

Small projects can turn passive study into active practice. A learner can start with a clear problem and gradually add features as understanding improves.

Keeping each project focused makes it easier to test, explain and improve. Documentation is also part of the learning process.

Key idea: finish small projects before expanding them.

Article 3: Thinking Clearly About AI Tools

AI tools can accelerate research and drafting, but users still need to check important results. Clear instructions and careful review improve the quality of the final work.

Good technology use combines automation with human judgment. The goal is not simply speed; it is useful and trustworthy output.

Key idea: review important AI-generated information before relying on it.
