Faculty Information

Department faculty are available during their designated office hours for academic guidance and doubt clearing sessions. Students must book a slot in advance through the department office.

Sequence Before a Practical Class

Students must follow these steps in order before entering the laboratory:

  1. Complete the pre-lab reading assigned for the session.
  2. Bring the signed lab record from the previous session.
  3. Report to the lab at least 5 minutes before the scheduled time.
  4. Wear the college ID card at all times inside the lab.
  5. Check in with the lab assistant before starting any experiment.

Classroom Rules

  • Maintain silence during lectures unless invited to speak.
  • Switch mobile phones to silent mode.
  • Keep the classroom clean and organized.
  • Submit assignments through the designated portal only.

Academic Rules

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to be eligible to sit for the semester examination.

Note: Internal Assessment marks will be uploaded by 15th September 2026.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A formal classroom session where a faculty member delivers theoretical content.
Lab
A practical session conducted in a laboratory to apply theoretical concepts.
Tutorial
A smaller interactive session focused on problem-solving and doubt clearing.
Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation conducted through quizzes, assignments and class tests.
Practical
A hands-on examination assessing a student's applied skills in a subject.
