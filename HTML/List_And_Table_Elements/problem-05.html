Annual College Fest 2026 - GLA University, Noida

Fest Introduction

GLA University, Noida proudly presents its Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of talent, culture and technology. The fest brings together students from across the country to compete, perform and showcase their skills through a wide range of events.

Registration Process

Follow these steps to register for any fest event:

  1. Visit the official fest registration portal.
  2. Create a participant account using your college email ID.
  3. Select the event(s) you wish to participate in.
  4. Pay the applicable registration fee online.
  5. Download your participant pass and event schedule.

Registrations close on 5th October 2026, so early registration is recommended.

Rules

  • All participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Registration is mandatory before participating in any event.
  • Participants must report at least 30 minutes before their event.
  • Decisions of the judges and organizing committee will be final.

Event Terms

Open Event
An event open to participants from any college or institution.
Team Event
An event that requires participation in a group of two or more members.
Individual Event
An event in which a single participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for an event before the deadline.
Coordinator
The faculty or student in charge of managing a specific event.
Final Round
The concluding round of a multi-stage competition where winners are decided.
