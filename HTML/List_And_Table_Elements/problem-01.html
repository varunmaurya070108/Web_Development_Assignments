University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

GLA University, Noida welcomes applications from students seeking admission into its undergraduate and postgraduate programs. This handbook explains the eligibility criteria, application process, required documents, important terms and the applicable fee structure for the upcoming academic session.

"We are committed to guiding every applicant through a transparent and student-friendly admission process." - Admission Office, GLA University, Noida

Eligibility

Applicants must have completed their qualifying examination from a recognized board or university with the minimum marks specified for their chosen program. Meeting the minimum eligibility criteria is mandatory for the application to be considered.

Application Process

Follow the steps below in the exact sequence to complete your application:

  1. Register on the official admission portal using a valid email address.
  2. Fill in personal, academic and program preference details.
  3. Upload scanned copies of required documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee online.
  5. Download the submitted application form for your records.
  6. Appear for the entrance test or interview if applicable to your program.

Applications must be submitted before the last date of 30th September 2026.

Required Documents

Keep the following documents ready before starting your application (order does not matter):

  • 10th and 12th grade mark sheets
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate (if applicable)
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Valid government-issued ID proof

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualification and marks required to apply for a given program.
Merit List
A ranked list of applicants prepared on the basis of academic performance or entrance test scores.
Counselling
A process in which shortlisted candidates choose their preferred course and campus based on merit rank.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to eligible students based on merit, need or category.
Registration
The initial step of creating an applicant account on the admission portal before filling the form.
