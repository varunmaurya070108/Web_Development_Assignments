Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

For first-time visitors, we recommend following this sequence to enjoy the full tasting experience:

  1. Begin with the Crispy Corn Chaat as a light starter.
  2. Follow with Paneer Tikka or Chicken Seekh Kebab.
  3. Move on to the signature Butter Chicken or Mutton Rogan Josh with naan.
  4. Refresh your palate with Fresh Lime Soda between courses.
  5. Finish the meal with Gulab Jamun or the Chocolate Brownie.
