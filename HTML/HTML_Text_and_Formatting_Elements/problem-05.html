Science Notes-

Chemistry-

Water has the chemical Formula H20

Carbon dioxide is represented as CO2

Oxygen gas is represented as O2

Sodium Chloride is represented as NaCl

Sulphuric Acid is represented as H2SO4

Subscript numbers show how many atoms of an element are present in a formula.

Physics-

The area of a square is measured in m2, while the volume of a cube can be measured in m3.

Scientific notation can represent very large or very small numbers, such as 3 × 108.

Velocity describes the rate of change of displacement with time.

Biology-

Photosynthesis is an important biological process in which green plants use light energy to help produce food.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.
