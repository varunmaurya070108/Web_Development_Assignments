Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their college ID card while on campus. The ID card may be required for entry into classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and other college facilities.
2. Students must maintain cleanliness on the college campus. Waste should be disposed of only in designated dustbins.
3. Students should use college property responsibly. Any damage to classroom furniture, laboratory equipment, or other facilities may result in appropriate disciplinary action.

Attendance

4. Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject. Students with attendance below the required percentage may not be allowed to appear for the final examination, according to college rules.
Students should regularly attend classes and avoid unnecessary absences.
Note: Students should contact the concerned faculty member in case of a genuine reason for extended absence.

Examination

5. Students must carry their valid college ID card during examinations.
6. Students must reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled starting time.
Mobile phones, smartwatches, and other unauthorized electronic devices are not permitted inside the examination hall.
Note: Students should carefully read the examination instructions before starting the examination.

Assignment Submission

7. Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.
Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive reduced marks or may not be accepted.
8. Students must submit assignments in the format and method specified by the concerned faculty member.
Note: Students should keep a copy of their submitted assignments for future reference.

Discipline

9. Students must behave respectfully with teachers, staff members, and other students.
10. Students must not engage in activities that disturb the academic environment of the college.
Note: Maintaining discipline helps create a positive and comfortable learning environment for everyone.

Important Notice

All students are expected to follow these rules and complete their academic responsibilities on time.
The rules may be updated by the institution when necessary. Students should regularly check official college notices for any changes.
