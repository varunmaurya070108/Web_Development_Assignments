NSA Ajit Doval Visits China for India-China Boundary Talks

Talks focus on maintaining peace along the India-China border

* India's National Security Advisor Ajit Doval arrived in Beijing on 24 August 2026 for a two-day visit.
He is scheduled to participate in the 25th round of Special Representatives' talks concerning the India-China boundary question.
* The discussions are expected to focus on the boundary issue and maintaining peace and stability between the two countries. The meeting is significant because India and China have been working to improve communication and manage differences along their disputed border.
* The talks are taking place in Beijing, with both sides expected to discuss ways to maintain stability and reduce tensions. Maintaining peace along the border remains an important priority for both countries.

European Union Announces Major Support for Ukraine

European leaders reaffirm support for Ukraine on its Independence Day

* Ukraine marked its 35th Independence Day on 24 August 2026 as the country continued to face the ongoing war with Russia. European leaders expressed support for Ukraine and its continued independence.
* European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen announced €6.1 billion in European Union military assistance for Ukraine. The support is expected to focus particularly on air and missile defence capabilities.
* The United Kingdom also announced plans to help Ukraine strengthen its long-range missile capabilities. European countries continue to emphasize their commitment to Ukraine's sovereignty and security.
