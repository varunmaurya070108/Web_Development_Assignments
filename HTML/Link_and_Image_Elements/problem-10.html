About Institution

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-focused educational campus located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The campus aims to provide students with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, practical skills, professional abilities, and industry awareness.

Principal Message

Dear students, your education is more than a collection of marks. Our aim is to help you develop curiosity, discipline and the ability to solve meaningful problems.

Academic Programs

Our major academic areas include Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Humanities and Applied Sciences. Each program combines theoretical foundations with practical activities.

Students studying science may explore concepts such as H2O chemistry and physical measurements. A computing student might work with a simple expression such as x2 + y2.

Campus Facilities

The campus includes classrooms, laboratories, a library, collaborative work areas and recreational spaces. Facilities are intended to support both focused study and group learning.

Student Life

Student life is an important part of the university experience. Clubs, cultural activities, sports and project teams give students opportunities to develop communication and teamwork skills.
