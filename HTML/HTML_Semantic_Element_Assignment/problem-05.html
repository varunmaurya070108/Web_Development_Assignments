Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a two-day event bringing together leading universities, employers and training institutes to help students and job seekers explore higher education, internships and placement opportunities in one place.

Our objectives are to bridge the gap between academia and industry, provide career guidance to attendees, and facilitate direct interaction between organizations and candidates. For general career guidance resources, visit the National Skill Development Corporation website.

Notice: Entry is free for all registered attendees; on-spot registration will also be available subject to capacity.

"This fair gives thousands of students direct access to opportunities they would otherwise struggle to find. It's one of the most impactful events we organize every year."

— Fair Organizing Committee Chairperson

Preparation Guide

  • Carry multiple printed copies of your resume
  • Dress in formal or business-casual attire
  • Research participating organizations in advance
  • Prepare a short self-introduction pitch
  • Bring a notepad to record contact details
  • Carry a valid photo ID for entry

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online and receive a confirmation with QR code.
  2. Arrive at the venue and check in at the registration desk.
  3. Visit organization booths relevant to your interest area.
  4. Attend scheduled sessions and workshops.
  5. Submit resumes or applications directly at booths.
  6. Collect follow-up information and next steps.

Terminology

Career Fair
An event where employers and candidates meet to explore job opportunities.
Internship
A short-term, supervised work experience, often for students or recent graduates.
Placement
The process of securing a job offer, often facilitated by an institution.
Scholarship
Financial support awarded to students based on merit or need.
Apprenticeship
A structured training program combining paid work with formal instruction.
