Key Terms

Consultation
A scheduled meeting with a doctor to discuss symptoms or health concerns.
Follow-up
A subsequent visit to review progress after initial treatment.
OPD
Outpatient Department — for patients who do not require hospital admission.
Appointment
A reserved time slot with a specific doctor or department.

How to Book an Appointment

  1. Select the department and preferred doctor.
  2. Choose an available date and time slot.
  3. Fill in your patient details in the form.
  4. Submit the request and await confirmation via SMS/email.
  5. Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled time with prior reports, if any.
