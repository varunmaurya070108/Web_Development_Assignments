About GLA University Noida

GLA University Noida is a premier institution committed to academic excellence, innovation and holistic development. With state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty and strong industry collaborations, the university offers a vibrant environment for undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral studies.

Our mission is to nurture critical thinkers and skilled professionals who are ready to contribute meaningfully to society. Learn more on the UGC official website.

Eligibility & Key Terms

Eligibility
Minimum qualifying marks required in the relevant board/entrance examination.
Merit List
A ranked list of candidates published based on entrance score or academic performance.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to meritorious or economically weaker candidates.
Application Fee
A non-refundable fee paid at the time of submitting the admission form.

Application Process

  1. Register on the admission portal with basic details.
  2. Fill the online application form and upload documents.
  3. Pay the application fee.
  4. Appear for the entrance test or submit qualifying scores.
  5. Check the merit list and complete counselling.
  6. Pay admission fee and confirm your seat.
