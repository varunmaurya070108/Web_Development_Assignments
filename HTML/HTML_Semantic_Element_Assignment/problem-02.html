About NimbusTech Solutions

NimbusTech Solutions is a fast-growing technology company building cloud infrastructure products used by enterprises worldwide. We believe in ownership, curiosity and craftsmanship as the pillars of great engineering culture.

Our employee value proposition is simple: meaningful work, transparent growth and genuine flexibility. Read more about our engineering culture on our public engineering blog.

Hiring Note: We are currently scaling our Platform Engineering and Cloud Security teams for the upcoming quarter.
