Destination Overview — Jaipur, The Pink City

Jaipur is a vibrant destination known for its forts, palaces & rich culture. Visitors can explore history < and > enjoy modern hospitality, all in one trip.

Travel Tips

Best time to visit is October to March. Carry sunscreen, comfortable shoes & a valid ID.
